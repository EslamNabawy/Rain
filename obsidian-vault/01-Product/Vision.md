# Vision

Rain is a quiet, premium private peer-to-peer messenger for people who already trust each other.

The product should make connection truth visible without turning the app into a network console. A user should understand:

- who they are talking to
- whether the peer is online
- whether a direct or relay path is active
- whether chat, file transfer, voice call, or video call is currently allowed
- what failed and what to do next

## Product Principles

- No public feed.
- No follower graph.
- Accepted friends only.
- One global active voice/video call.
- Calls and files should never conflict silently.
- Manual disconnect means do not reconnect until the user explicitly presses Connect.
- Closed app means offline until a future push/background service is implemented.
